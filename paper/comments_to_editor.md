# Comments to the Editor

Dear Editor,

I am submitting an original research paper, "Emergent and evolvable resource-trading
mutualism in Symbio-Grid: an agent-based model of plant–fungi symbiosis under mutation and
phosphorus stress," for consideration in Applications of Modelling and Simulation.

The paper presents a spatial agent-based model and a reproducible simulation study of how a
resource-trading mutualism between plant and fungi agents emerges, evolves, and collapses
under varying mutation and phosphorus conditions. It falls within the journal's scope of
modelling and simulation in the sciences and of modelling-and-simulation tools: agent-based
modelling is a standard simulation methodology, and this work applies it to an ecological
system while releasing the model and all experiments as open, reproducible software. The
model is described following the ODD protocol, and every reported result is reproducible
from a fixed random seed via the accompanying script.

I would be grateful for your guidance on two points. First, please confirm that an
agent-based-modelling study of this kind fits the journal's scope. Second, the manuscript is
a focused, exploratory study and is shorter than a typical full-length article; I would
welcome your advice on whether it is best handled as a regular paper or, if the journal
offers one, a shorter format.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under
consideration elsewhere. The work received no external funding, and I declare no competing
interests. All software and data are openly available.

Thank you for considering this submission.
